Cookie policy

This site uses Google Analytics cookies only with your yes. This page says what is collected, for how long, and how to change your mind.

Last updated 27 November 2026

Who looks after this data

Patravana Resort Khao Yai owns this website and decides how its visit data is used. O2 Design Studio, which looks after the site for the resort, set up Google Analytics and reads its reports on the resort's behalf. For anything about your personal data, call +66 88 504 1888 or write to info@patravanaresort.com.

What we use Google Analytics for

We use Google Analytics, a service of Google, to count visits and see which parts of the site are read, so that we can improve it. It runs only if you choose Accept when the site asks, or later under Cookie settings at the foot of the page. Until then nothing is loaded from Google and no Google Analytics cookie is set; if you never answer, nothing is. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the site does not ask and Google Analytics stays off.

The site's own visit count, described in the privacy note on the main page, sets no cookie and is not part of this choice.

The cookies

With your yes, Google Analytics sets two cookies on this site: _ga, a random number that tells one browser from another, and _ga_QV62419KMY, which keeps the current visit together. Each ends 180 days after your last visit. Your answer itself is kept in this browser, not in a cookie, for 180 days; then the site asks again.

What Google receives

Google receives the address of the page you open, with nothing after it (no codes), the address of the site you came from, the time, your browser, kind of device, screen size and language, and an approximate location that Google works out from your IP address; Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses. It also learns whether you scroll to near the end of the page, and that you pressed Book, LINE, phone, email or the map, without the link itself.

Nothing you choose or type on the site is sent: not your dates, guests or room, and not the words of an enquiry. Nothing is used for advertising: advertising storage, Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off.

Legal basis, processor and how long it is kept

We use Google Analytics on the basis of your consent under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019). Google processes this for us under its data-processing terms, and may do so outside Thailand, including in the United States. We look at it as totals and trends in Google Analytics' reports. Google deletes the detailed record of visits after 2 months; the totals in its standard reports remain.

Changing your mind, and your rights

To withdraw your yes, open Cookie settings at the foot of the page and choose Decline: Google Analytics stops at once and its cookies on this site are deleted. You can also delete cookies in your browser's settings, or install Google's own opt-out add-on.

You may ask to see, delete or object to the use of your personal data by contacting us as above, and you may complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee.

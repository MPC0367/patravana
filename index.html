Around the grounds

Facilities

00/ 08

Eight markers, one real photograph. Hover a facility below to find it on the map.

On the map

The pitched-roof wings hold the sixty-four keys, every one with its own balcony facing the lawn or the lake. The resort's own caption on the photograph points here. The tiered lawn steps down toward the water, built for group nights and gatherings of any size, with the hills as the backdrop. The lake sits at the heart of the estate, with the white PATRAVANA letters on its near bank and wooden piers for the quiet hours. A timber deck runs along the water beside the kitchen, where breakfast and dinner come with the lake a few steps away. Phu Yai Lee, the resort's restaurant, labelled on the photograph by the resort itself. Thai cooking with a few international detours, indoors and out, 150 seats. The rock-edged lagoon pool, tucked between the kitchen and the suite lawns, with a fitness room nearby for the diligent. A loop path circles the great lawn between the wings and the lake. The resort's own drone photographs show guests cycling it. Off Mittraphap Road, u-turn at kilometre 39. The drive curls in past the gate and the parking sits beside the first wing.

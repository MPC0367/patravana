The place

Air

Part of the Phu Patra estate in Phayayen, in the hills of Pak Chong.

The itinerary here is short. Wake up to the hills, walk the lawns down to the lake, and let the day set its own pace.

  • 64suites and rooms in three suite types, each with a balcony
  • 20kmto Khao Yai National Park, by the resort's own listing

Around the grounds

Facilities

The whole resort from the air, as a map. Choose a marker to see that place, then go straight to it.

Eight places on one aerial photograph. Choose a facility below to find it on the map.

On the map

The pitched-roof wings hold the sixty-four suites and rooms, each with its own balcony. The tiered lawn steps down toward the water, with the hills as the backdrop. Tell the team what you are planning and they will advise on the setup. The lake sits at the heart of the estate, with the white PATRAVANA letters on its near bank and wooden piers for the quiet hours. A timber deck runs along the water beside the kitchen, where breakfast and dinner come with the lake a few steps away. Phu Yai Lee, the resort's restaurant. Thai cooking with a few international dishes, indoors and outdoors, up to 150 seats. The rock-edged lagoon pool, tucked between the kitchen and the suite lawns, with a fitness room nearby for the diligent. A loop path circles the great lawn between the wings and the lake, made for an unhurried evening walk. The entrance is off Mittraphap Road in Phayayen, with free parking on site. For the approach that suits your direction of travel, use the map pin under Contact.

02

What time are check-in and check-out?

Please ask the team for the current check-in and check-out times, and about arriving early or leaving late.

+66 88 504 1888LINE @patravana

03

Can children stay, and is there an extra bed?

Each suite is set up for two adults, and one extra bed can be added for a third. Children are added on the resort's booking page, which shows what each room can take for your party.

04

Is breakfast included?

It depends on the rate. The resort's booking page offers rates with breakfast and room-only rates, and names what each one includes before you confirm. Breakfast is served daily at Phu Yai Lee, 7:00 to 10:00.

05

When does the restaurant serve?

Breakfast is served every day, 7:00 to 10:00. Lunch and dinner are served Wednesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 21:00. On Monday and Tuesday there is breakfast only. For a table, call +66 84 088 7785.

06

How do I get there, and is there parking?

The resort is in Phayayen, Pak Chong, off Mittraphap Road. Open the map pin for directions from where you are. Parking is free on site.

Open in Google Maps

07

Is the resort accessible?

Please tell the team what would make your stay comfortable, such as step-free routes or a particular room location, and they will advise before you book. There is no need to share medical details.

08

Can the resort host a group or a seminar?

Yes. The seminar hall beside the resort is 280 m² and divides into two rooms. With the full hall it takes 220 guests in theatre rows, 168 in classroom rows, 120 for a banquet and 64 in a U-shape.

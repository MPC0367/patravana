The place

Fresh Air

Part of the Phu Patra estate, nestled in the valleys of Pak Chong in the foothills of the Khao Yai National Park.

Wake up among the clouds, breathe air from a national park, and let the hills set the pace of the day.

  • 64suites and rooms in three suite types, each with a balcony
  • 20kmto Khao Yai National Park, by the resort's own listing
  • 280m²of seminar hall nearby, offsite, up to 220 guests

Slow mornings, wide lawns, and nowhere you have to be.

On the map

    The pitched-roof wings hold the sixty-four suites and rooms, each with its own balcony. The tiered lawn steps down toward the water, with the hills as the backdrop. Tell the team what you are planning and they will advise on the setup. The lake sits at the heart of the estate, with the white PATRAVANA letters on its near bank and wooden piers for the quiet hours. A timber deck runs along the water beside the kitchen, where breakfast and dinner come with the lake a few steps away. Phu Yai Lee, the resort's restaurant. Thai cooking with a few international dishes, indoors and outdoors, up to 150 seats. The rock-edged lagoon pool, tucked between the kitchen and the suite lawns, with a fitness room nearby. A loop path circles the great lawn between the wings and the lake, made for an unhurried evening walk. The entrance is off Mittraphap Road in Phayayen, with free parking on site. For the approach that suits your direction of travel, use the map pin under Contact.

    How to get here

    Patravana is in Phayayen, Pak Chong, in Nakhon Ratchasima province. Choose how you are travelling.

    By car

    From Bangkok, take Phahonyothin Road (Route 1) north past Rangsit toward Saraburi, then turn onto Mittraphap Road (Route 2) toward Pak Chong and Nakhon Ratchasima.

    The resort is off Mittraphap Road in Phayayen, and the way in depends on your direction of travel, so let the map pin guide the last stretch. Travel time depends on where you start, the traffic and your stops. Parking is free on site.

    Navigate with Google Maps

    By train

    No train runs to the resort itself, so the last part of the journey is by road. The resort's own directions use Muak Lek. The railway now lists two stations there, Muak Lek and Muak Lek New, so check which one your train stops at, and where it leaves from in Bangkok, in the current State Railway of Thailand timetable.

    Contact the resort before you travel to confirm the best arrival point for your train and to arrange the onward journey.

    State Railway of Thailand timetables and ticketsSRT call centre 1690

    By coach or minivan

    If you plan to come by coach or minivan, check with your operator where it leaves from and whether it can set you down near Pak Chong or Muak Lek, as not every service stops along the way.

    Then contact the resort to arrange the last stretch to Patravana.

    From the airport

    Bangkok has two airports, Suvarnabhumi (BKK) and Don Mueang (DMK). From either one, continue by car as above, or ask the resort about transfer options before you travel, including availability, notice and any charge.

    Suvarnabhumi AirportDon Mueang Airport transport

    03

    Can children stay, and is there an extra bed?

    Each suite is set up for two adults, and one extra bed can be added for a third. Children are added on the resort's booking page, which shows what each room can take for your party.

    04

    Is breakfast included?

    It depends on the rate. The resort's booking page offers rates with breakfast and room-only rates, and names what each one includes before you confirm. Breakfast is served daily at Phu Yai Lee, 7:00 to 10:00.

    05

    When does the restaurant serve?

    Breakfast is served every day, 7:00 to 10:00. Lunch and dinner are served Wednesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 21:00. On Monday and Tuesday there is breakfast only. For a table, call +66 84 088 7785.

    06

    How do I get there, and is there parking?

    The resort is in Phayayen, Pak Chong, off Mittraphap Road. Open the map pin for directions from where you are. Parking is free on site.

    Travelling without a car? Contact the resort before making travel arrangements to ask about current transfer or pickup options.

    07

    Is the resort accessible?

    Please tell the team what would make your stay comfortable, such as step-free routes or a particular room location, and they will advise before you book. There is no need to share medical details.

    08

    Can the resort host a group or a seminar?

    Yes. The seminar hall, Patra Hall, is offsite, close to the resort: 280 m², drawn as 30 × 10.40 m, dividing into room A (12.35 × 10.40 m) and room B (14.15 × 10.40 m). With the full hall it takes 220 guests in theatre rows, 168 in classroom rows, 120 for a banquet at 12 round tables and 64 in a U-shape.
